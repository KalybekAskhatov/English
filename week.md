## Сцена A: Who I am (пн, вт, пт)

Вопросы партнёра:

1. What is your name?
2. Where are you from?
3. Where are you based now?
4. What is your job?
5. Are you a frontend developer or a full-stack developer?
6. Is your company big or small?
7. What are you good at?
8. What are you interested in outside of work?

Заготовки, из которых в субботу выписываются словосочетания: I'm a frontend developer. · I'm from … · I'm based in … · I'm … years old. · I'm good at … · I'm interested in …

## Сцена B: My team and my tools (ср, чт, пт)

Вопросы партнёра:

1. How many people are on your team?
2. Who is your team lead?
3. Are there any designers or testers on your team?
4. Is your team remote or in the office?
5. What is your main framework?
6. What is your favorite tool, and why?
7. Is VS Code your main editor?
8. Are your daily meetings long or short?

Заготовки, из которых в субботу выписываются словосочетания: There are … people on my team. · My team lead is … · We're a remote team. · My main framework is Angular. · My favorite tool is … because … · Our meetings are short.
