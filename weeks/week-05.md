## Сцена A: My skills and tech stack (пн, вт, пт)

Вопросы партнёра:

1. What can you do well as a frontend developer?
2. Which frameworks and languages can you work with?
3. Can you write unit tests? What do you use?
4. What do you have to do every day at work?
5. What can't you do yet?
6. What should a good frontend developer know?
7. Which of your skills are you most confident in, and which one do you want to improve? *(сложнее)*
8. If you had to pick one tool you couldn't work without, what would it be? *(сложнее)*

Заготовки, из которых в субботу выписываются словосочетания: I can build … with Angular. · I'm comfortable with … · I have to review pull requests every day. · I can't … yet, but I'm learning. · A good developer should … · I want to get better at …

## Сцена B: Asking for help and a code review (ср, чт, пт)

Ролевая сцена: ИИ — коллега, ты просишь помощи и ревью. Начинаешь ты.

Вопросы партнёра:

1. Hi! What do you need help with?
2. Sure. Can you show me the code?
3. What did you try?
4. I'm a bit busy right now. Can it wait until after lunch?
5. OK. Should I look at the whole pull request or only one file?
6. I think you should rename this function. What do you think?
7. I'm not sure I follow. Could you walk me through what this part is supposed to do? *(сложнее)*
8. I've left a few comments. Can you take care of them by the end of the day? *(сложнее)*

Заготовки, из которых в субботу выписываются словосочетания: Could you take a look at my pull request? · Could you help me with this? · I'm stuck on … · It can wait until after lunch. · I see your point, but … · I'll fix it today.
