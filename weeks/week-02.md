## Сцена A: My workday (пн, вт, пт)

Вопросы партнёра:

1. What time do you start work?
2. Do you work from home or from the office?
3. What do you do first in the morning?
4. Do you have a daily standup? What do you say there?
5. How many hours a day do you write code?
6. Who do you talk to most during the day?
7. Walk me through a typical day: what does it look like from start to finish? *(сложнее)*
8. What usually slows you down at work? *(сложнее)*

Заготовки, из которых в субботу выписываются словосочетания: I start work at … · I work from home. · First, I check my messages. · We have a daily standup at … · I write code for about … hours a day. · I usually finish at …

## Сцена B: What my project does (ср, чт, пт)

Вопросы партнёра:

1. What does your project do?
2. Who uses it?
3. What does the main page show?
4. Which part do you work on?
5. Does the app work on mobile phones?
6. How often does your team release?
7. What problem does it solve for the customer? *(сложнее)*
8. If I open the app for the first time, what do I see and what can I do? *(сложнее)*

Заготовки, из которых в субботу выписываются словосочетания: It's a web app for … · Our users are … · It lets users … · I work on the … page. · We release every two weeks. · It works on desktop and mobile.
