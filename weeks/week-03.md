## Сцена A: What I did yesterday (пн, вт, пт)

Вопросы партнёра:

1. Were you at work yesterday?
2. What was your main task yesterday?
3. Was it easy or difficult?
4. Who were you on a call with?
5. What did you finish?
6. Was there a problem? What was it?
7. How was your day overall, and what took most of your time? *(сложнее)*
8. What did you get done, and what did you have to put off? *(сложнее)*

Заготовки, из которых в субботу выписываются словосочетания: Yesterday I worked on … · I fixed a bug in … · I finished the … · I was on a call with … · It was harder than I expected. · There was a problem with …

## Сцена B: My previous job and education (ср, чт, пт)

1. Where did you study?
2. What did you study?
3. What was your first job?
4. How long were you there?
5. What did you work with at that job?
6. Why did you leave?
7. What was the most useful thing you learned there? *(сложнее)*
8. How did you get into frontend development in the first place? *(сложнее)*

Заготовки, из которых в субботу выписываются словосочетания: I studied … at … · My first job was at … · I was there for … years. · I worked with … · I left because I wanted to … · I learned a lot about …
